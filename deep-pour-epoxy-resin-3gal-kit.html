      Product Questions

      What is included in the 3gal kit?+

      The kit contains three 1gal jugs: two jugs of Part A resin and one jug of Part B hardener. Total contents are A 2gal + B 1gal. MOQ is 1 set.

      How do I measure the 2:1 ratio?+

      Measure two equal volumes of Part A resin for every one volume of Part B hardener. For example, 200mL A requires 100mL B. Mix thoroughly, scraping the sides and base. Do not use a weight ratio unless the current product instructions explicitly provide one.

      What can I make with this deep pour resin?+

      Discuss river tables, wood-and-resin art, dried-flower preservation and thick decorative castings with our team. Use fully dried inclusions and confirm wood preparation, sealing and mold suitability in the technical guide.

      How deep can I pour at one time?+

      The supplied specifications show 2–4 inches, subject to temperature, batch volume and mold geometry. Confirm the permitted layer depth and mixed quantity in the current TDS for your exact project before pouring.

      What are the working and curing times?+

      The ArFlair 2:1 deep-pour technical sheet lists 60–80 minutes of working time at a recommended 24–29°C, 8–12 hours to a tack-free surface, and 48–72 hours to full cure. Temperature, casting mass and geometry can change the timing; verify a representative pour before production.

      Can I order private-label packaging and technical documents?+

      Contact sales@arflairresin.com or WhatsApp with the product name, quantity and packaging requirements. Request the current instructions, TDS and Part A / Part B SDS for this 3gal 2:1 deep pour kit.

      Does the 3 gal pack define the permitted batch size?+

      No. The pack contains 2 gal Part A and 1 gal Part B. Permitted mixed batch volume and layer depth must be confirmed for the selected formulation, temperature and casting geometry.
