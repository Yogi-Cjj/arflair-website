      Choosing your materials

      Which resin should I choose for my project?+

      Start with the application: coatings, small castings, deeper pours or light-cured details. Share your intended dimensions, finish and production process with us so we can discuss a suitable product and provide its technical information.

      What is the difference between epoxy and UV resin?+

      Two-part epoxy cures after its resin and hardener are mixed. UV resin needs a compatible curing light and suitable light access. The right choice depends on the shape, depth, color and workflow of your project.

      Can I request product specifications before ordering?+

      Yes. Tell us which material you are considering and request the current technical data sheet and safety data sheet for that specific product. Review them before sampling or production.

      Do you offer pigments and other DIY supplies?+

      You can inquire about mica powders, resin colorants, decorative additions and kit accessories. Let us know the resin system and intended effect so compatibility can be discussed.

      Working with resin

      What mixing ratio should I use?+

      Use the ratio on the exact product label and technical data sheet. Ratios by weight and by volume are not interchangeable. Do not use instructions from a different resin formulation.

      How long does resin take to cure?+

      Working time, demolding time and full cure depend on the formulation and processing conditions. Check the product instructions for temperature, batch size and curing requirements before planning your schedule.

      Why are there bubbles in my casting?+

      Air can enter during mixing or come from porous inclusions. Check your mixing method, materials and working conditions against the product guide. Share photos and batch details with us for a more useful troubleshooting discussion.

      What should I do if a piece stays sticky?+

      Check the mixing ratio, measurement method, mixing process and curing conditions. Keep the piece out of use and share the product name, batch number and processing details. Follow the product guidance for handling uncured material.

      Can I add flowers, colors or decorative pieces?+

      Compatibility and moisture can affect the result. Start with a small sample using dry, suitable inclusions and colorants recommended for your resin system. Approve the sample before scaling up.

      Where can I find handling and safety information?+

      Read the product-specific safety data sheet and label before use. They set out handling, protective equipment, ventilation, storage and disposal requirements. Ask us for the relevant documents for the product you plan to order.

      DIY kits & private label

      Can a kit be developed for my brand?+

      Share your target customer, project idea, sales market and budget range through our quote page. We can discuss kit contents, branding and packaging options for your brief.

      Can I create a seasonal collection?+

      Yes—start with a theme, such as Halloween or Christmas, and the craft experience you want to offer. Send reference ideas and your intended launch date so materials, kit contents and packaging can be discussed together.

      Can I customize the packaging?+

      Include your preferred pack format, artwork status, label language and market in your inquiry. Available options, artwork requirements and order quantities are confirmed for the specific project.

      Can I review a sample before bulk production?+

      Request a sample plan with your inquiry. Sample availability, customization scope, costs and timing need to be confirmed before proceeding.

      Can you help me select kit contents?+

      Tell us the intended project, skill level and customer group. We can discuss a material and accessory list; final contents and instructions should be reviewed through your sample approval process.

      Wholesale & ordering

      What is the minimum order quantity?+

      Minimum quantities depend on the product, pack size and customization. Tell us the items and estimated quantities you need so we can confirm the options for your project.

      How do I request a quotation?+

      Open Get Quote, enter your product interests, quantities and destination, and prepare your request. You can then review the message in WhatsApp and send it to our team.

      What should I include in my inquiry?+

      Product type, estimated quantity, destination, packaging needs and target date are useful starting points. A reference link or a short project description can help clarify the request.

      Can I combine different products in one inquiry?+

      Yes. List the materials or kits and the approximate quantity for each. Availability, packing and shipping arrangements will be confirmed in the quotation.

      Delivery & order support

      Do you ship to my country?+

      Share your delivery country, postal code and product list. Shipping options depend on the goods and destination and will be confirmed with your quotation.

      How long will my order take?+

      Timing depends on stock, customization, sample approval, quantity and transport. Ask for a project-specific schedule and confirm it before placing an order.

      How do I report an order issue?+

      Contact us on WhatsApp with your order reference, product and batch details, photos and a short description. Keep the packaging and affected goods while the next steps are agreed.

      Can I ask about documentation for my market?+

      Include your destination and documentation requirements at the inquiry stage. Ask for confirmation of the documents available for the exact product before ordering; requirements vary by product and market.
