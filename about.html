      OUR PRODUCT SCOPE

      Materials and supplies for a coordinated range.

      Source the material itself, combine complementary supplies, or develop an assembled DIY kit around a specific project.

      Epoxy Resin

      Two-part resin and hardener products for decorative casting and craft collections. Choose from the stated pack options and discuss the formulation suited to your application.

      UV Resin

      UV resin supplies for charms, jewelry and small craft projects, with pack sizes from 100g to 25kg. Confirm the curing setup and product instructions for your selected specification.

      Resin Casting Powder

      Casting powder for decorative trays, vases and other molded pieces. Pack options include 1kg, 2kg, 3kg, 4kg, 5kg and 6kg.

      Colors & Pigments

      Mica pigments and color selections to complement your resin range. Discuss colors, individual pack sizes and coordinated assortments.

      Silicone Molds

      Coaster, tray and decorative casting molds. Confirm cavity dimensions, shape, material compatibility and order quantity before selection.

      Reusable Mold Maker

      Remeltable mold-making material in a 454g pack for custom craft shapes. Discuss use instructions and compatibility with your intended casting material.

      QUALITY, PACKING & ORDER READINESS

      Make the important details clear before production.

      Reliable sourcing starts with a clear specification. Review the material, pack size, application and packaging details together so both sides understand the order being discussed.

      Product specification & sample review

      Share what you want to make and the conditions in which the product will be used. Discuss the formulation or material specification, evaluate a sample where appropriate and confirm the agreed requirements before scaling up.

      Technical information & documentation

      Tell us which technical, safety or market-specific documents your business requires. Documentation and any testing requirements are confirmed for the selected product; they should be reviewed alongside its instructions.

      Labels, contents & outer cartons

      Check your product name, declared size, artwork, included components and carton quantities as part of the packing brief. For a DIY kit, define the complete contents list and instruction requirements.

      Destination & delivery planning

      Share the delivery country, destination details and target timeline. Production scheduling, freight, packing requirements and commercial terms are confirmed in the quotation discussion.

      Questions about our factory and your order.

      Where is the ArFlair factory located?+

      Our factory is in Foshan, China. ArFlair was established in 2019, and the facility covers approximately 3,000+ m² with 60+ employees and 3 production lines.

      Which products can I discuss with ArFlair?+
