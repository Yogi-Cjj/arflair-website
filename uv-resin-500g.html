      500 g UV Resin Specifications

      ArFlair 500 g clear hard-type UV resin is a single-component formula for small silicone mold casting. Use it to make coordinated runs of resin jewelry, pressed-flower pendants, charms and letter keychains. Its documented 2,000–3,000 mPa·s viscosity is suited to pouring thin layers into detailed molds. Cure each layer under a dedicated UV resin lamp following the resin and lamp instructions, and demold only after the piece is fully cured.

      Net weight500 g
      Viscosity2,000–3,000 mPa·s at 25°C (UV-3660 TDS)
      Cured hardnessShore D 80–85 (UV-3660 TDS)
      Curing methodUV light, 365–400 nm (UV-3660 TDS)
      Reference light intensity800–1,200 mW/cm² (UV-3660 TDS)
      Surface cure3–5 seconds under TDS test conditions
      Complete cure60–90 seconds for a 1–2 mm layer under UV-3660 TDS reference conditions; thicker or shaded layers need separate validation
      MixingSingle component; no hardener required

      The UV-3660 TDS figures use a 365–400 nm UV source, 800–1,200 mW/cm² intensity and a 20–30°C environment. The TDS lists 60–90 seconds for complete curing of a 1–2 mm layer under its reference conditions. Thicker, pigmented or shadowed layers need longer exposure and a sample test. Confirm that the quoted bottle uses the UV-3660 formulation and request its current TDS before setting production times. For a wholesale sample, record lamp wavelength and output, layer thickness, pigment loading and the fully cured result. Read the UV resin sample guide →

      Product Questions

      Do I need to mix this UV resin with hardener?+

      No. It is a single-component UV resin. Pour a thin layer and cure it with a dedicated UV resin lamp.

      What can I make with the 500 g bottle?+

      Use it for small molds and jewelry findings, including pendants, earrings, keychains and charms.

      What kind of lamp do I need?+

      Use a dedicated UV resin curing lamp and follow the resin and lamp instructions.

      Can I request a wholesale or private-label quote?+

      Yes. Share your quantity and packaging requirements with ArFlair when requesting a quote.

      Why test a clear sample before a colored jewelry batch?+

      A clear control helps separate the basic resin-and-lamp setup from the effects of pigment or inclusions. Approve the final colored combination separately under the selected product instructions.
