      Start with the finished product and casting geometry. A pack size describes the supplied quantity; it does not establish the permitted mixing batch or pour depth. Approve the exact resin, substrate, color and process on a representative sample before scaling your order.

      Private-label epoxy resin: prepare a useful buying brief

      1. Define the range. List the coating or casting application, selected kit sizes, estimated quantities and destination country.
      2. Review the sample. Record the intended substrate, coating thickness or casting depth, mixed quantity and room conditions. Request the current product instructions, TDS and Part A / Part B SDS.
      3. Confirm the packaging. Discuss bottle or jug format, label artwork, instruction language, retail carton and outer-carton requirements. Custom-packaging minimums should be stated in the quotation.
      4. Approve commercial details. Confirm the agreed contents, sample charges, production schedule, shipping method, freight estimate and delivery terms before placing the order.

      Information for US and European buyers

      ArFlair is based in Foshan, China. Include the destination country and postal code so the team can assess shipping options for your actual order. Ask for the quoted dispatch origin, freight costs and delivery terms; destination taxes and import arrangements should be clarified separately. A product listing is not confirmation of local stock or a fixed delivery date.

      Wholesale epoxy resin questions

      Which kit should I review first?

      For an art-coating range, begin with the 1 gal kit and your actual substrate. For a river-table cavity, review the 3 gal deep-pour kit with measured void dimensions. The selected formulation must suit the application; the largest pack is not automatically the best choice.

      Can the resin be supplied with my brand and packaging?

      Private-label options are available for discussion. Send the selected product, order quantity, label design, packaging format and market. Ask our team to confirm the sample, artwork process and applicable minimums for that configuration.

      Which technical documents can I request?

      Product-specific TDS and SDS, also called MSDS, are available on request for epoxy resin. Request documents for the quoted formulation. CE-related conformity documents are available for the applicable variants they cover; confirm the exact product model and intended EU use.

      How do I get a wholesale quotation?

      Use the inquiry form to select the product and estimated quantity, then add your application, pack format and delivery destination. A useful quotation should identify the resin specification, included components, packaging and commercial terms.
