        BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

        A few useful answers.

        Still have a question? Include it in your request.

        What should I include in a quote request?

        Start with the product category, estimated quantity, destination and packaging needs. For a DIY kit, describe the finished project and the contents you would like to include.

        Can I request private-label packaging?

        Yes. Choose private-label or custom DIY kit packaging and share your proposed label, pack sizes and design direction. Artwork requirements and commercial details are reviewed with the team.

        Can I discuss samples before a bulk order?

        Select “Requesting samples” as your project stage. The team can discuss sample options, specifications, availability and any applicable costs for your selected materials.

        How are price, MOQ and delivery confirmed?

        These depend on the selected product, quantity, packaging and destination. They are confirmed in the quotation discussion; this page does not calculate a final price or promise a fixed delivery time.

        How do I send reference files?

        Use the optional file picker to prepare a list of images or PDFs. After opening WhatsApp, attach those files in the chat. Selecting a file here does not upload or send it.
