      Description

      ArFlair Chameleon Powder is a color-shifting pigment powder for epoxy resin art. It gives resin jewelry and decorative castings a metallic finish that changes with the viewing angle, lighting, and base color.

      • 3 g powder format: Net weight is 3 g / 0.105 oz. See the color guide below for examples of the finish and ask about current shade availability.
      • Metallic color shift: Turn a finished piece under the light to see different tones. Compare a small sample over light and dark bases before choosing a finish for your batch.
      • Apply to silicone molds: Brush a thin, even layer inside a clean mold, pour compatible epoxy resin, and demold after the resin has fully cured according to its instructions.
      • Resin jewelry and crafts: Use this chameleon pigment powder for earrings, pendants, shell charms, bookmarks, and small decorative castings.
      • Wholesale and private label: Request a quote with your preferred shades, order quantity, and packaging requirements.

      Product Questions

      How do I use chameleon powder in a resin mold?+

      Brush a light layer inside a clean mold, pour compatible resin, and demold after the resin is fully cured.

      Why does the color look different from another angle?+

      The metallic color shift varies with viewing angle, lighting, and base color. Test a small casting before making a batch.

      What can I make with this pigment?+

      Use it to add a color shift to resin earrings, pendants, shell charms, bookmarks, and small decorative castings.
