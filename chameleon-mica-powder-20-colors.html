      Description

      ArFlair’s 20-color chameleon mica powder set gives epoxy resin jewelry and decorative castings a shimmering finish that changes with the viewing angle.

      • 20 shades in one boxed set: Each jar contains 3 g / 0.105 oz of mica pigment, for a total net weight of 60 g.
      • Shimmer that changes with the angle: Tilt a cured piece to see the color shift. Lighting and base color affect the result, so test a small casting before making a batch.
      • Apply to silicone molds: Brush a thin, even layer of powder inside a clean mold, pour compatible clear epoxy resin, and demold after the resin has fully cured.
      • Resin jewelry and crafts: Use this color-shifting pigment for pendants, earrings, keychains, bookmarks, trinket dishes, and decorative castings.
      • Wholesale inquiries: Share your order quantity and packaging requirements when requesting a quote for the boxed set.
