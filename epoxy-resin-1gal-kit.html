      Product Questions

      What is included in this kit?+

      One 2L jug of Part A resin and one 2L jug of Part B hardener, with 4L combined volume. Contact us to confirm the packing configuration for your order.

      What is the mixing ratio?+

      Mix Part A and Part B in equal volumes: A:B = 1:1 by volume. Do not use a 1:1 weight ratio. Mix thoroughly, including the sides and bottom of the container.

      How long are the working and curing times?+

      Working time is approximately 40 minutes and curing time is approximately 8–24 hours. Temperature, batch size and project conditions affect these times. Follow the current product instructions for curing and handling.

      Is it suitable for coatings, coasters and flower preservation?+

      Discuss decorative coatings, coasters, jewelry, resin art or dried-flower preservation with our team to confirm the appropriate formulation and pour depth. Use fully dried flowers and test a small sample before a full project. Deep or large castings may require another formulation.

      What packaging and custom branding options are available?+

      Packaging options include 1kg, 1gal, 2gal, 5kg, 10kg, 50kg and 200kg. Custom branding, labels and packaging are available for discussion. Tell us your pack size, quantity and branding requirements for a quotation.

      How can I get instructions, the TDS and the SDS?+

      Email sales@arflairresin.com or contact us on WhatsApp. Specify the 1 gal Epoxy Resin Kit for Art Coatings and request the current instructions, TDS and SDS for both Part A and Part B.
