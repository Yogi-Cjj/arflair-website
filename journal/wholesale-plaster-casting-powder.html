      Wholesale Plaster Casting Powder: How to Choose a Kit and Request Samples

      Start with 1 kg to screen colors and small shapes, 3 kg to repeat samples for a coordinated collection, or 5 kg to test several batches with larger molds or more cavities. Choose the pack around the amount of powder your trial will use.

      By ArFlair

      Estimate the powder needed for your sample run

      Run one representative batch with the mold you plan to use. Record the dry powder weight and the number of pieces that meet your finish requirements. Divide the weight by the number of accepted pieces to estimate powder use per accepted piece.

      Multiply that figure by your target sample count. Allow for any additional color or finish trials, then choose a kit size. Use the current instructions for the exact product to measure powder and water. For help evaluating the result, see our casting powder surface and project planning guide.

      What to include in a wholesale sample request

      • Mold and intended use. Share the piece type, largest cavity dimensions and the number of pieces you want to sample.
      • Finish. State your preferred color and surface appearance, and whether you will paint or seal the piece. A reference photo helps define what you want to approve.
      • Order volume and packaging. Share the estimated number of kits per order, preferred pack weight, and any private-label or instruction-language requirements.
      • Destination and timing. Include the destination country and postal code, your sample deadline, and any product documentation needed for review.

      Ask which sample configuration is available and confirm the minimum order quantity for your chosen pack and packaging. Agree on the sample details before comparing production quotes.
