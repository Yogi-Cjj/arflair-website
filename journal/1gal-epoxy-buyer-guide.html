      BUYER GUIDES / EPOXY RESIN

      1 gal epoxy resin: a buyer's sample checklist

      A useful wholesale sample does more than look clear in the bottle. Check the ratio, coating method, working window and finish on your own substrate before choosing a pack and placing a production order.

      By the ArFlair technical team · 4 min read

      IN THIS GUIDE

      01   Confirm the exact kit02   Test the working window03   Approve the coating sample04   Send a useful buying brief← All stories

      Bring these to the sample test

      • The current TDS and Part A and Part B SDS for the quoted formulation
      • Actual substrate, coating area and target wet-film thickness
      • Volume measuring tools, mixing containers and the protection specified in the SDS
      • A record sheet for temperature, batch size, working time and cure observations
      01

      Confirm the exact kit

      The supplied OS-820AB technical sheet specifies Part A to Part B at 1:1 by volume. Volume and weight ratios are not interchangeable. The current ArFlair 1 gal epoxy kit page lists 0.5 gal Part A and 0.5 gal Part B; confirm the filled volume, formulation and packaging in your quotation. Ask for the current instructions and both component SDS before use.

      02

      Test the working window

      OS-820AB lists about 35 minutes of usable time at 25°C for a 50 g mix and a 24-hour room-temperature cure at 25°C. These are reference conditions, not guaranteed times for a large production batch. Prepare a representative sample using the intended mixed quantity and room conditions. Record when the material begins to thicken and when the finished piece can be handled according to the product instructions.

      03

      Approve the coating sample

      Apply the resin to the actual artwork, wood or other intended substrate. Check spread, bubbles, surface appearance, edge behavior and finishing after the specified cure. If the project includes dried flowers or pigments, test the exact inclusion and color loading. Keep the sample and your mixing record as the approval reference for repeat orders. Discuss any thick casting separately; a coating formula should not be assumed suitable for a deep pour.

      04

      Send a useful buying brief

      Tell the supplier your end use, coating area and thickness, substrate, room temperature, target pack size, estimated number of sets, destination and label or private-label needs. Ask which formulation and packaging the quote covers, what sample is available, and which current TDS and SDS apply. Use the 1 gal kit inquiry form to submit the product and estimated quantity together.

      TAKE THIS WITH YOU

      Approve the exact resin, substrate and process as one sample before scaling the order.
