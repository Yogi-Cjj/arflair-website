      MATERIAL GUIDES / MATERIAL SELECTION

      Casting powder projects: plan the surface before the pour

      Casting powders can produce a different look from transparent resin. Begin with the desired surface, the shape and the exact material system before planning a decorative range.

      By the ArFlair editorial team · 2 min read

      IN THIS GUIDE

      01   Identify the material first02   Make a small working sample03   Separate demolding from final readiness04   Test the intended finish← All stories

      Before you begin

      • A selected casting powder and its product instructions
      • The specified liquid binder or water
      • Accurate measuring tools and a compatible casting form
      • A finish or sealer confirmed suitable for that material
      01

      Identify the material first

      “Casting powder” covers different formulations. Confirm the composition, binder and measuring basis from the supplier. A ratio or demolding time for one powder should not be applied to another product with a similar name.

      02

      Make a small working sample

      Prepare the ingredients using the documented ratio and mixing sequence. Control dust as described in the SDS and use the specified protective measures. Keep the form on a level surface and follow the recommended filling method.

      03

      Separate demolding from final readiness

      A piece that can be removed from its form may still need additional drying or curing before finishing. Follow the material’s full schedule and check when sanding, painting or sealing is permitted.

      04

      Test the intended finish

      Try color, paint and a compatible sealer on a sample before approving a collection. Assess the result against the actual use; do not assume a decorative casting is suitable for food contact, prolonged water exposure or outdoor service.

      TAKE THIS WITH YOU

      The specific formulation determines the process and the suitable end use.

      For material processing, use the current TDS and SDS for the exact product you select. Mixing ratios, cure conditions and suitable applications vary by formulation.
