      FOR BRANDS / COLLECTION PLANNING

      From project idea to a private-label DIY kit

      A useful DIY kit helps a customer complete one clear project. Define that experience first, then choose the materials, instructions and packaging around it.

      By the ArFlair editorial team · 2 min read

      IN THIS GUIDE

      01   Write the customer outcome02   Build the bill of materials03   Make the instructions match the contents04   Review the complete sample← All stories

      Before you begin

      • A target customer and one main project
      • A provisional bill of materials
      • Packaging dimensions and label requirements
      • A sample review checklist
      01

      Write the customer outcome

      Describe what someone should be able to make and how many pieces the kit is intended to produce. Decide who the instructions are for. This prevents accessories from accumulating without a role in the project.

      02

      Build the bill of materials

      List each resin component, decoration, tool and consumable with a proposed quantity. Check that the supplied resin quantity suits the project and its documented batch limits. Distinguish included items from tools the customer must provide.

      03

      Make the instructions match the contents

      Use the exact resin’s current processing instructions and safety information. Illustrate the preparation, measuring, mixing and curing sequence. Have a tester work through the draft using only the listed contents and required extra tools.

      04

      Review the complete sample

      Assess the finished result alongside bottle labels, leak protection, internal packing and the outer carton. Confirm quantities, artwork, commercial terms and the approval sample with the supplier before placing a production order.

      TAKE THIS WITH YOU

      Approve the customer experience, not just the appearance of the box.

      For material processing, use the current TDS and SDS for the exact product you select. Mixing ratios, cure conditions and suitable applications vary by formulation.
