      Product Questions

      What is the minimum order quantity?+

      The minimum order for the 1kg kit is 1 kit. For other pack sizes or custom-branded orders, contact our team to confirm the minimum quantity and quotation.

      What is included in the 1kg kit?+

      The kit contains one bottle of Part A epoxy resin and one bottle of Part B hardener, with a combined net weight of 1kg.

      What is the mixing ratio by volume?+

      Mix Part A resin and Part B hardener at a 1:1 ratio by volume. Measure equal volumes, then mix thoroughly, scraping the sides and bottom of the container. Do not substitute a 1:1 weight ratio.

      How long are the working time and curing time?+

      The typical working time is 30–40 minutes; the TDS gives approximately 35 minutes at 25°C for a 50g batch. Typical curing time is 8–24 hours, depending on temperature, batch size and project geometry. The TDS specifies 24 hours at 25°C for a 2g test sample. Follow the current TDS for your selected formulation; working time and curing time are different stages.

      Can I use it for coatings, coasters and flower preservation?+

      Discuss your decorative coating, coaster or dried-flower preservation project with our team so we can confirm the appropriate formulation. Use completely dried flowers, test compatibility on a small sample, and confirm the recommended pour depth and layer thickness before casting. Large or deep flower-preservation projects may require a different formulation.

      What packaging sizes and private-label options are available?+

      Packaging options include 1kg, 1gal, 2gal, 5kg, 10kg, 50kg and 200kg. Custom branding, label artwork and packaging can be discussed. Tell us your required pack size, quantity and branding requirements so we can confirm the configuration and quotation. Gallon sizes are volume specifications; kilogram sizes are weight specifications.

      How can I obtain instructions, the TDS and the SDS?+

      Request the product instructions, Technical Data Sheet (TDS) and Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for your selected formulation from our team. Email sales@arflairresin.com or contact us on WhatsApp. Include the product name and pack size, and request the Part A and Part B SDS before use.
