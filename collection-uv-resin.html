      BUYER'S SOURCING GUIDE

      Buying UV resin for jewelry and small castings

      UV resin is commonly selected for small pendants, charms and decorative details. The result depends on the lamp, layer thickness, pigments and mold. Test the exact combination you plan to use rather than relying on a single curing-time claim.

      What to include in your request

      • Intended item, mold depth and whether the finish will be clear or pigmented.
      • Lamp type and wavelength, production volume, pack size and label requirements.
      • Requested samples, technical information and destination-market documentation.

      Confirm before placing an order

      Check the selected resin’s lamp compatibility, curing instructions, surface finish, storage guidance, MOQ and packaging options with a current product sheet and sample trial.

      Compare the 250 g, 500 g and 1,000 g options, and see our UV charm project guide.

      Product documents: Product-specific TDS and SDS (also called MSDS) are available on request for UV resin. Check lamp and curing conditions in the selected product’s current TDS.

      CE documents: CE-related conformity documents are available for the applicable UV resin variants they cover. Request the document for the exact product model and intended EU use.
