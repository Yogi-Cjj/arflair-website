      UV Resin Technical Specifications & OEM Capabilities

      ArFlair UV Resin is a single-component, solvent-free photopolymer resin that cures completely within 60 to 180 seconds under standard 36W UV light or natural sunlight, delivering diamond clarity with zero shrink-wrinkling.

      Technical Parameter Specification / Manufacturing Standard
      Curing Wavelength365 nm - 405 nm (Optimal with 36W - 48W UV/LED nail lamps or craft lamps)
      Curing Speed60 - 120 seconds per thin layer (1-2mm); 3 - 5 mins for thicker jewelry bezels
      Viscosity RangeLow (1,500 cP) for fine detail bezels; High (4,500 cP) for dome coatings
      Shore D Hardness84 - 88 Shore D (Glassy rigid finish)
      Odor & EmissionsLow odor formula, zero VOCs, eco-friendly compliant
      Standard Packaging25g, 60g, 100g, 200g, 250g, 500g, 1000g squeeze bottles with precision nozzles
      Custom Color & GlittersClear transparent, pastel tints, pre-mixed glitter/glow-in-the-dark blends

      Frequently Asked Sourcing & Customization Questions

      Q: Why does UV resin feel sticky after curing?

      A: Stickiness is caused by oxygen inhibition on the surface. Using a 36W+ 395nm UV lamp for a full 2 minutes or applying a fast alcohol wipe eliminates all tackiness.

      Q: Can you supply private-label UV resin with customized bottles?

      A: Yes, we offer custom black/matte opaque UV-blocking bottles with your logo, barcode, safety warnings and custom packaging boxes.
