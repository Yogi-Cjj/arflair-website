      BUYER'S SOURCING GUIDE

      Choose resin pigments with a sample palette

      Color appearance changes with resin type, dose, layer thickness, lighting and background. Request a physical sample and test it in your own resin system before approving a wholesale color range.

      Which pigment format fits your range?

      • Chameleon mica powders: Compare the 10-color, 3 g per jar set and the 20-color, 3 g per jar set for shifting metallic effects.
      • Standard and glow colors: Review the mixed pigment powder set for its listed color assortment and pack weights. Confirm which colors provide a glow effect.
      • Liquid resin pigments: The four-color ocean-wave set contains 4 × 50 mL bottles for epoxy resin projects.

      Can I order pigment powders in bulk or with my own label?

      Send the product, desired quantity, target pack weight, color assortment and label artwork requirements. Ask our team to confirm available bulk and private-label configurations, MOQ, sample arrangements and lead time. A listed retail set does not establish the MOQ for a custom pack.

      Can the same pigment be used in epoxy and UV resin?

      Confirm compatibility for the exact resin and pigment combination. Test dispersion, appearance and cure in the intended layer thickness; do not assume the same dosage works across different resin systems. Start with our resin color palette guide and epoxy vs UV resin comparison.

      What to include in your request

      • Resin system, intended color effect and finished product.
      • Target palette, pack size, labeling and order quantity.
      • Required safety or market-specific documents for the intended use.

      Confirm before placing an order

      Confirm the selected pigment’s compatibility, dispersion, color consistency and documentation with the supplier and your own sample test. Do not assume a craft pigment is suitable for skin or food contact.
