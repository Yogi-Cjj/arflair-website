      1000 g UV Resin Specifications

      ArFlair 1000 g clear hard-type UV resin is a single-component formula for small silicone mold casting. The 1 kg jug is sized for repeat pours in jewelry workshops, craft studios and small production runs. Use it for botanical pendants, earrings, charms and keychains. Its 2,000 cP medium viscosity is suited to pouring thin layers into detailed molds. Cure each layer under a dedicated UV resin lamp following the resin and lamp instructions, and demold only after the piece is fully cured.

      Net weight1000 g
      Viscosity2,000–3,000 mPa·s at 25°C (UV-3660 TDS)
      Cure shrinkageUnder 2%
      Cured hardnessShore D 80–85 (UV-3660 TDS)
      OdorVery low
      Curing methodUV light, 365–400 nm (UV-3660 TDS)
      Reference light intensity800–1,200 mW/cm² (UV-3660 TDS)
      Surface cure3–5 seconds under TDS test conditions
      Complete cure10–30 seconds for a layer ≤0.2 mm under TDS test conditions; thicker layers need longer
      MixingSingle component; no hardener required

      The UV-3660 TDS figures use a 365–400 nm UV source, 800–1,200 mW/cm² intensity and a 20–30°C environment. The 10–30 second complete-cure figure applies only to layers no thicker than 0.2 mm. Thicker, pigmented or shadowed layers need longer exposure and a sample test. Confirm that the quoted bottle uses the UV-3660 formulation and request its current TDS before setting production times.
