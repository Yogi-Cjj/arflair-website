      Description

      Create layered ocean colors and crisp white surf with ArFlair Ocean Wave Epoxy Resin Pigment. This four-color pigment paste set is designed for epoxy resin seascapes, coasters, decorative trays, and coastal wood art.

      • Four colors for ocean effects: Use Dark Blue for deep water, Sea Blue for shallows, White for wave crests and foam, and Black for shadows and contrast.
      • Fine tips for controlled dispensing: The squeeze bottles let you add pigment gradually and adjust each shade before pouring.
      • Build your seascape in layers: Prepare the epoxy according to the resin manufacturer’s instructions, then tint separate portions for your water, surf, and accents. Test the color and cure on a small sample before pouring your full piece.
      • Package includes: Four 50 mL bottles, one each in Dark Blue, Sea Blue, Black, and White (200 mL total).
