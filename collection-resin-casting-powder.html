      WHOLESALE SELECTION & ORDER PLANNING

      Craft casting plaster for decorative product ranges

      These white plaster casting powder kits are intended for decorative trays, coasters, ornaments, figurines and DIY craft projects. Select them around the finished shape, batch process and pack presentation. This range is described for craft casting; it is not presented as construction plaster or wall-finishing material.

      Mixing and sample conditions

      The listed kits use a 3:1 powder-to-water ratio by weight: for example, 300 g powder with 100 g water. The product pages list approximately 25–30 minutes to demold and approximately 2 hours to full cure, depending on casting size and conditions. Follow the current selected-kit instructions and validate the actual mold and environment before planning a batch schedule.

      The cured finish is matte and porous, not waterproof. If a finished item needs a sealer or will be used around moisture, test the selected finish and intended use separately. See the casting powder surface and project planning guide.

      Plan a wholesale or private-label plaster kit

      Compare the 1 kg, 3 kg and 5 kg kits and learn what to include in a sample request in our wholesale plaster casting powder buying guide. Use the bulk quote checklist to compare kit contents, carton weight and delivery terms.

      1. Specify the project. Share the finished-item dimensions, mold configuration and desired color or surface effect.
      2. Choose the contents. Identify the 1 kg, 3 kg or 5 kg format and any required pigments, tools, molds or instructions. Confirm the complete item list in the quotation.
      3. Approve a representative sample. Record powder, water and pigment quantities. Inspect release, detail, the dry finish and packaging fit before repeating the process.
      4. Confirm the supply plan. State the number of kits, destination country and postal code, label language and packaging requirements. Request the applicable MOQ, sample charges, production timing and freight quotation.

      Wholesale casting powder questions

      Which pack should I choose for trays and coasters?

      Review the 3 kg decor kit for matching batches or the 5 kg studio kit for repeat use. Use the 1 kg starter format to evaluate a new color or small shape. Confirm actual consumption from your mold instead of assuming a fixed yield per bucket.

      How many finished pieces can one kit make?

      Yield depends on the cavity volume, selected mixing ratio and process losses. Share the mold dimensions or measured consumption from a sample so the required order quantity can be assessed.

      Can I request my own brand or a custom craft kit?

      Send the powder pack size, pigment assortment, tools, label artwork and estimated quantity. Private-label and custom DIY kit configurations are confirmed during project review; the standard kit listing does not establish custom-packaging minimums.

      Are paints, sealers and molds included?

      The listed kits include the powder and accessories shown in their item lists. Acrylic paint, sealer and molds are not automatically included. Ask for an itemized quotation if your planned kit requires additional materials.
