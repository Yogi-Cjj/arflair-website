      Product Questions

      What comes in the kit?+

      A 5 kg bucket of white plaster casting powder, five water-based dropper pigments and one silicone spatula.

      How do I measure the mix?+

      Weigh three parts powder to one part water. For example, use 300 g powder with 100 g water.

      When can I take the casting out of the mold?+

      Demold gently after about 25–30 minutes. Full cure takes about 2 hours, depending on casting size and conditions.

      How can I add color?+

      Tint the wet mix with the included plaster colorants, or paint a fully dry cast with separately sourced water-based acrylic paint.

      Are the finished casts waterproof?+

      No. Cured plaster is porous. A compatible sealer can help protect the surface, but does not guarantee waterproofing.

      Are acrylic paint and sealer included?+

      No. Acrylic paints, brushes and sealers are not part of this kit.
