      Description

      ArFlair Glow-in-the-Dark Pigment Powder adds luminous accents to epoxy resin crafts. Charge finished pieces under a suitable light source, then move them into darkness to reveal the glow.

      • 12 colors plus 3 glow colors: The 12-color palette includes Blue, Sky Blue, Blue Green, Green, Yellow Green, Yellow, Purple, Fuchsia, Magenta, Coral, Salmon, and Gold.
      • For resin jewelry and DIY décor: Use luminous pigment powder in pendants, earrings, keychains, dice, coasters, and decorative resin castings.
      • Mix, cast, and charge: Follow your resin manufacturer’s mixing and curing directions. Stir in a small amount of powder, test a sample, and charge the cured piece before viewing in darkness.
      • Glow varies by shade: Light source, exposure, and the finished resin piece all affect the result. Use the color chart as a visual guide.
