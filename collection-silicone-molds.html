      BUYER'S SOURCING GUIDE

      Source silicone molds by shape and casting process

      The right mold depends on cavity dimensions, surface detail, release requirements and the material being cast. Share the finished piece or a drawing so the mold specification can be reviewed for your project.

      What to include in your request

      • Finished-part dimensions, cavity count, wall thickness and desired surface finish.
      • Casting material, expected process temperature, order quantity and packaging needs.
      • For a custom shape, a sketch, 3D file or physical reference and sample approval criteria.

      Confirm before placing an order

      Confirm material grade, temperature limits, expected reuse, food-contact suitability where relevant, tooling cost, MOQ and production timing from the specification and supporting documents for the chosen mold.
